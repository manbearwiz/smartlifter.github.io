# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy covers the Smart Lifter Lite app for Android.

## What the app collects

Nothing. Smart Lifter has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send anything over
the internet. It doesn't ask for any permissions beyond vibrating the phone during a timed workout.

## What stays on your phone

Your lifts, your workout history and your settings are stored on your phone, inside the app. They
stay there until you delete them or uninstall the app.

## Backups

- **Backup files.** You can save a backup of your lifts, history and settings to a file, or share
  one through Android's share sheet. You choose where it goes (your phone, Google Drive, an email,
  and so on), and the app never sends it anywhere on its own. Once saved or shared, the file is
  handled by whatever app or service you sent it to.
- **Android backup.** If you have Android's own backup turned on, Android may include the app's data
  in your device backup to your Google account. That is part of Android and is covered by
  [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Google Play

Google Play may collect information such as crash reports, device model and Android version, and
share summaries of it with developers. That is collected by Google, not by the app, under
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Deleting your data

Delete lifts or history entries in the app, or uninstall it to remove everything it stores. Backup
files you saved are yours to delete wherever you saved them.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
